Expand description

An in-process listener. Called after the operation’s record is saved and every lock is released, in registration order; cannot veto; never receives a handle to the core (open a second TxCore on the same state dir if you need one). Hand slow work to your own thread.

Order is per core: another process’s events reach you only through the log, and log order across processes is write order, not causal order.

A panicking sink is contained with catch_unwind: the operation already happened and the log line is already written. The default panic hook still prints the panic to stderr (core installs no hook; that is the front end’s call), the sink’s own Mutexes are poisoned afterwards, and nothing is contained under panic = "abort".

Closures (|e: &Event| …) and Arc<impl EventSink> are sinks. The closure impl rules out a future blanket impl for Box<T> or &T; box your sink when registering it.
